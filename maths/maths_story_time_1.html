📖 What is this game about?
You will listen to a short maths story.
Your job is to find the equation that matches what happened in the story — showing whether to add or subtract!

🎮 How to play
  • 🔊Press the orange button to hear the story read aloud — you can listen as many times as you like!
  • 🧠Think carefully: are the objects being joined together, or taken away?
  • 👆Tap the equation that matches the story. There are 4 choices each time!
